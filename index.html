01. About Me

I am a researcher working at the nexus of AI, medical data science, computer engineering, and health science. My work is driven by a deep conviction that mathematics is not an abstraction — it is a tool for building more interpretable, stable, and trustworthy models in biomedical settings.

My research spans academic and industry environments, with collaborations at UC Berkeley, Texas A&M University, Woxsen University, and QNu Labs. I work on healthcare analytics, deep learning development, model optimization, and ODE-based modeling of dynamic systems.

Beyond applied AI, I explore theoretical foundations — multiset representations, combinatorial structures, and differential equation–driven learning — to better understand how intelligent systems behave and fail.

My long-term goal: contribute to research that meaningfully connects mathematics, computation, and health.
